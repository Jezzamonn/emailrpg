WELCOME to Email RPG

This is an exciting new game where YOU get to be part of the emailing action, and all you gotta do is send those emails!

That's right! All it needs are some simple EMAILS from you...

Before we get into the story, Let's get things set up: What do you look like??

Here are the allowed options:

Skin tone (in a row): light, medium, dark

Hair type: short, ponytail, bald

Hair color: blonde, brown, black (not needed if ya bald!)

Clothes color: red, blue, green

Reply with your choices (e.g. short brown hair, light skin, blue clothes or bald, dark skin, red clothes) to enter the world!
