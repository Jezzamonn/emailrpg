OK,, here's the story so far:

You find yourself in a strange world. It seems like a nice peaceful beach. BUT things are not all what they seem...

Have you ever received an email that tells you to forward it on to someone else? You've always forwarded it on, right?? But have you ever wondered... what happens if you don't? Well....... read on...

For you see, this place is haunted by the spirits of those who have NEVER forwarded a single chain email!! Beyond this beach, you'll find the 4 spirits who need your help. To enter their world, you'll need to explore the 4 portals and free them all. And maybe, there will be a special reward...
